The selected and adapted metadata in scripts/data/orcatopia-heritage-200.json,
the additional catalog facts for entries 101-300, and their factual translations
are provided under CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes: selected 200 properties, shortened titles,
converted country codes, rounded coordinates, selected named component points
where appropriate, translated labels, and added original concise descriptions.
For distributed properties, markers may represent the wider property rather than
the exact facade illustrated. Each entry links to its UNESCO property page.
No source photographs, maps, illustrations, videos or logos are bundled.
Original miniature vector artwork, animation code and fictional garden designs
are separately covered by the repository MIT license.
