The architectural spread is deliberate: four countries in Asia, two in Africa and two in the Americas. Casapueblo is a building developed over decades; its inclusion does not imply a single recent opening date. Black Star Gate is a separate monument from Independence Arch. The existing country groupings are preserved.

Coordinates were retrieved from Wikidata P625 on 2026-11-08; entity URLs live in the catalog. They are illustrative map points. National Museum of Qatar has multiple coordinate statements; the catalog uses the first retrieved point, rounded to six decimals. Baiterek uses its Wikidata monument point, not the different tourism-directory point.

The added collection allocation is five Discovery and three Treasure entries, with existing entries unchanged. It is a provisional editorial game allocation, not an independently validated application of the numerical rarity rubric. No new numerical recognition scores are asserted. Research supports identity, location and architectural descriptions, not measured popularity. Reassessment of the full ranking remains separate.

The catalog grows to 59 landmarks / 56 countries and the decoration catalog to 126. Current allocation: 30 Discovery, 19 Treasure, 7 Marvel, 3 Wonder. Tier draw rates remain 70/23/6/1; uniform selection within a tier means each item’s conditional chance changes as its pool expands.
